# Refund Policy

- Orders that arrive more than 30 minutes late are eligible for a partial refund or credit.
- Refunds for missing or wrong items are issued in full for the affected item.
- Refund requests must be made within 24 hours of delivery.
- Refunds above 200 EGP require manager approval and may take up to 3 business days.
- If an order is cancelled before the restaurant confirms it, a full refund is automatic.
